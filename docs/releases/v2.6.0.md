WhisperDrop 2.6.0 makes Parakeet v3 the default speech model, writes notes while you speak with one sentence per paragraph, lets you pause a note and rename it by clicking its title, and adds local writing tools and in-app updates.

## Install

Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later. Coming from 2.5.0 or earlier, download the DMG once and replace the app; your notes, models and settings stay. From 2.6.0 on, use **WhisperDrop 2 > Check for Updates…** or **Settings > General > App updates** instead of downloading again.

The app is ad hoc signed and not notarized, so macOS may block the first launch. If you trust this build, open it once, then choose **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**. After replacing the app, macOS may ask again for Accessibility and Input Monitoring.

## Parakeet v3 by default

- Parakeet v3 is the default model and is no longer experimental. It covers 25 European languages, including Italian and English, and is fast enough to write while you speak.
- If your main model was Turbo and Parakeet is already downloaded, the app switches to Parakeet once. Choosing another model afterwards is kept.
- Whisper models are listed as legacy models in **Settings > Models**. When your language is not one of Parakeet's 25, an installed Whisper model is used automatically, Turbo first, and Settings shows which one.

## Notes

- With Parakeet, notes are no longer cut into one-minute chunks. Words join the transcript a few seconds after you say them, and every sentence starts a new paragraph. Words that may still change are shown in grey.
- **Pause** stops recording without closing the note. Paused time is not recorded and the timer stops; **Resume** continues in the same note and audio files. While paused, the model can unload to free memory.
- Click a note's title to rename it, while recording or after saving. Press Return to save or Escape to cancel.
- With a legacy Whisper model, notes still appear after a pause or about a minute of speech.

## Writing tools

- Writing tools open in the main window. Fix grammar, make wording natural, adjust tone, rewrite, summarize, organize notes or make text concise. Edit the presets or add your own in **Settings > Writing**.
- Select text in another app and press Control + Option + D for a compact review panel. Replace is offered only when the original selection can be verified.
- Completed notes and transcripts have a **Writing** menu. Summaries and revisions are saved as separate versions; the original transcript stays.
- A review panel can open after dictation. Automatic note summaries are optional and off by default.
- Text models run locally: LFM2.5 2.6B (default, 1.59 GB) or MiniCPM5 1B (experimental, 688 MB), chosen in **Settings > Models > Text editing**. Review every suggestion; both models can make mistakes, especially in Italian.

## In-app updates

- Updates are signed and verified before installation, and install only after you confirm. Daily checks are optional and off by default.
- Updating waits for recordings, dictation, transcriptions, imports and writing actions to finish. Models, notes and settings are kept.
