# WhisperDrop 2.6.2

This update stops WhisperDrop from degrading Bluetooth headphone audio.

## Bluetooth headphones

- Music and video in Bluetooth headphones no longer sound muffled while WhisperDrop is open. Earlier versions kept the headset in its low-quality speaking mode whenever it was the Mac's default input, even with no dictation or note running.
- Dictation and notes record from the Mac's built-in microphone when the default input is a Bluetooth headset, and leave the headset in its music mode. A Mac without a built-in microphone still uses the headset microphone, only while recording.
- Recording follows a change of microphone, or a microphone that disconnects, without restarting the note.
- The last tenth of a second of a dictation is no longer cut off.

## Install

Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later. Replace the app in Applications. Notes, settings and downloaded models stay outside the app bundle.

The app is locally signed and not notarized. A first downloaded copy may require **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**.

## Validation

The debug and release suites passed 167 tests each, with 12 optional checks skipped. On an M4 Mac with macOS 27 and connected Bluetooth headphones, the headset kept its 44.1 kHz playback format while capture was idle, running and stopped, and capture followed a changed and a removed microphone. Diagnosis and limits are in `docs/bluetooth-headset-playback.md`. A Mac without a built-in microphone, USB and multichannel inputs, and a physical dictation shortcut were not verified.
